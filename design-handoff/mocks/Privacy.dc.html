What this site does not do

  • No cookies. Not even a "necessary" one, so there is no cookie banner.
  • No third-party requests. Fonts, images and code are all served from this domain — your visit is never disclosed to Google, a CDN, or anyone else.
  • No advertising, no fingerprinting, no social media embeds or share-button trackers.
  • No accounts, no comments platform, no data sold or shared — there is nothing to sell.

Analytics

I like to know which posts people find useful, so this site runs a self-hosted copy of an open source analytics tool on my own server. It counts page views in aggregate — no cookies, no personal identifiers, no cross-site tracking — and the numbers never leave my infrastructure. If your browser sends a "do not track" signal, it is honoured.

The contact form

The contact form sends one email, to me, through a form handler running on my own server. Your name, email address and message exist in my inbox and nowhere else. I use them only to reply to you, and I delete the thread when the conversation is done. Nothing is added to a mailing list — if I ever start a newsletter it will be strictly opt-in.

Server logs

Like every web server, mine keeps short-lived technical logs to detect abuse and keep the site running. IP addresses in those logs are anonymised, and logs are deleted automatically after 14 days.

Links elsewhere

When a post links to another site, that site's own privacy practices apply from the moment you click. I link to privacy-respecting places where a choice exists.

Your rights, and questions

Under UK GDPR you can ask me what I hold about you (almost certainly: at most, an email you sent me), ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Just drop me a line — no form, no process, I'll just do it.
