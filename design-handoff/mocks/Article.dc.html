A new name: becoming Sīlavāpi

After four months in the mountains of Spain, I came home with a new name and a very full heart.

Text size

In April I travelled to a retreat centre in the mountains of Spain, and in July I came home as somebody with two names. Getting ordained into the Triratna Buddhist Order is not a small thing — it is the culmination of more than a decade of practice, and the beginning of something much longer.

The name I was given is Sīlavāpi. Names in our tradition are chosen by one's preceptor, and they are part description, part aspiration — a quality you carry, and a quality to grow into. Mine is rooted in sīla: ethical practice, the ground everything else stands on.

A name is not a costume. It is a direction of travel.

So what changes? Practically: this website, my email address, and how I introduce myself at conferences (that will take some practice). What doesn't change: my work as Project Lead for Mautic, my writing here, my trusteeship at Breathworks, and my slightly unreasonable relationship with ultramarathons.

If you've known me as Ruth for years — that's still fine. Both names are true. But this site now lives at silavapi.co.uk, and I'd love it if you updated your bookmarks and your RSS reader. Everything from the old site has moved with me, and every old link should bring you to the right place.
