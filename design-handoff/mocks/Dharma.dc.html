Sīlavāpi is the name I was given at ordination. It is rooted in sīla — ethical practice — which feels right: for me the dharma has never been separate from work, community, or how a piece of software treats the people who use it.

Away from the cushion I'm a trustee of Breathworks, a charity teaching mindfulness-based approaches to living with pain and illness — work that sits very close to my own experience of living with Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome.
