abstract: >-
  Join this open call with Ruth Cheesley, Mautic Project Lead, once a fortnight. We’ll have a short presentation and discussion on a specific topic and then the floor will be opened to all attendees for open discussion. This time we’re celebrating Maintainer Month(External link) and we’ll be diving into how Mautic is maintained as an open source project. We’ll explore how the community is structured from the governance perspective, the different teams and what they look after, and the kinds of tasks that they work on. We’ll also cover how you can suggest a feature, and the process that goes through to become part of Mautic’s core. Come along, ask product questions, suggest feature requests, lodge complaints, offer praise, share ideas, discuss recent blog posts, or talk about good or bad experiences using Mautic. Anything that’s on your mind is fair game. I’m here to listen, share, and be available to help in any way I can.
