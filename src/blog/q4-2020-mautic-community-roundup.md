Another quarter passes and we mark a year of progress in the Mautic Community since we held our first in-person community sprint in November 2019 at Contribution Day in Amsterdam.

 Let’s kick off a roundup of all the awesome things that have happened this quarter!

Read the previous reports here for [Q3 2020](https://www.mautic.org/blog/community/q3-2020-mautic-community-roundup), [Q2 2020](https://www.mautic.org/blog/community/q2-2020-mautic-community-roundup) and [Q1 2020](https://www.mautic.org/blog/community/q1-2020-mautic-community-round).

## Our first Mautic World Conference

November 2020 saw us run the first ever World Conference for the Mautic Community, [MautiCon 2020](https://mauticon.mautic.org/). We were determined to hold an event that was welcoming to our worldwide community and as inclusive as possible.

With six tracks running simultaneously throughout the day, 17 sponsors, 51 speakers and 65 sessions across seven languages with 333 attendees, by all accounts it was a huge success!

Furthermore, the event generated just over $13,000 for the Mautic Community which has been transferred over to our [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/mautic), in addition to the $595 that was received from Spreadshirt commissions on purchases from our [Official Mautic Swag Shop](https://shop.spreadshirt.net/mautic-community-swag-shop/).

This absolutely would not have been possible without the support of our [amazing sponsors](https://mauticon.mautic.org/sponsors) and the MautiCon 2020 Working Group, who put in endless hours of work behind the scenes to make the event happen. We would also like to extend a big thank you to the team from [Veertly](https://www.veertly.com/) who expertly hosted the event on their platform.

If you missed some of the talks from MautiCon, don’t worry - they are all available on our YouTube channel [here](https://www.youtube.com/c/MauticOrg) - make sure that you subscribe to get notified when we release new videos!

## Product updates

We fixed 93 bugs this quarter in our patch and minor releases, all of which were released on time in accordance with our release schedule.

We also merged 30 features and enhancements this quarter including releasing [Mautic 3.2](https://www.mautic.org/blog/community/mautic-3-2-growing-together) , bringing improvements both to the product itself and to our behind-the-scenes tooling which has massively simplified the process of making a new release.

With Mautic 2 coming to the [end of security support in December 2020](https://www.mautic.org/blog/community/goodbye-mautic-2-its-been-good-know-you) , we are expecting to see a significant reduction in the 2,000+ active Mautic instances running the 2.x version in the coming months.

Despite a lot of hard work we were not able to hit our target of getting the open Pull Requests and Issues under 100 by the end of the year - but we are still chasing the goal!

## Conclusion

This has been a very full quarter with a huge amount of energy being put into our first ever Mautic World Conference and launching our plans for the future of Mautic.

Alongside that we have kept pace with our release schedule and continue to see growth across the community - all of this at the height of the global pandemic - quite an achievement!

I am excited for what the future holds and look forward to an exciting year ahead!
