I help established open source projects and foundations navigate the complex challenges of growth, governance and sustainability.

## What I do

Over the past decade I have led Mautic through corporate acquisition, a spin-out to independence, governance restructuring and financial recovery. I have built funding models, resolved community conflicts, grown contributor bases, and made the difficult decisions that keep an open source project viable long term. Now I have the chance to bring that experience to other projects facing similar challenges.

I work with established open source projects and foundations on the problems that come with maturity. How do you scale community governance when informal structures stop working? How do you build sustainable revenue without compromising your values? How do you navigate corporate relationships while staying independent? How do you grow your contributor base when you are competing with well-funded alternatives?

These are not theoretical questions for me. I have worked through all of them, sometimes successfully and sometimes through hard lessons, and that experience shapes how I approach consulting work.

## Areas where I can help

- **Governance and decision-making** - creating or refactoring governance models that actually work for your project's size and maturity, and moving from informal leadership to structured processes without losing what makes your community special.
- **Financial sustainability** - developing revenue models, funding pathways and membership structures that align with open source values. I have built these from scratch and fixed them when they broke.
- **Community growth and contributor retention** - attracting contributors is one challenge; keeping them engaged and preventing burnout is another. I have worked on both.
- **Marketing and positioning** - helping projects articulate their value and reach their audience, with particular strength in privacy- and sovereignty-focused positioning.
- **Strategic planning and recovery** - making difficult decisions, restructuring operations, and navigating crises. I have led Mautic through acquisition, spin-out and financial recovery.
- **Conflict resolution and community health** - addressing tensions before they become destructive, and building governance that handles disagreement constructively.

## How I work

My approach is informed by Buddhist principles around interdependence, skilful means, and working with things as they are rather than as we wish them to be. That means starting from your actual situation rather than an ideal one, considering the whole ecosystem rather than isolated problems, and finding pragmatic solutions that acknowledge your constraints while still moving toward your values.

I don't believe in one-size-fits-all frameworks. What works for a project with fifty active contributors won't work for one with five hundred, and what works for a foundation-backed project won't work for an independent community. I start by understanding your specific context, then develop approaches that fit.

## Experience and background

I have led Mautic since 2020, through some of the most challenging periods in the project's history. Before that I served on the Joomla Community Leadership Team, and I have worked in open source community building for over eighteen years. Along the way I have navigated corporate acquisition, managed a spin-out to independence, created governance structures from scratch, built funding models, led through financial crisis, and handled the operational realities of keeping a global open source project running.

I bring that hands-on experience to consulting work. When we talk about governance, sustainability or community conflict, I am speaking from direct experience rather than case studies.

## Working together

I work on a consulting basis at rates starting at £1,500 per day. Engagements usually start with some discovery work to understand your situation, then move into the specific areas where you need support. Some projects need ongoing strategic guidance; others need intensive help with a particular challenge like governance restructuring or financial planning; some need facilitation for a difficult conversation or decision.

I am particularly interested in working with projects that share values around privacy, digital sovereignty, and building alternatives to proprietary tools. If your project is trying to prove that open source can compete with well-funded proprietary alternatives while holding on to its ethical principles, we will probably work well together.
