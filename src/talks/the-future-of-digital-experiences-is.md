abstract: >-
  How will advances in technology change the way that we deliver and build digital experiences in the future? How can we, as technology providers, influence the digital experience of consumers across multiple touch points, without juggling several tools and creating a tech stack that limits our ability to scale? In this session Ruth Cheesley, Mautic Project Lead, will share a vision for digital experiences of the future. We will talk about some of the common obstacles faced by technology providers in delivering seamless digital experiences today, and the impact of a poor digital experience for the business and the consumer. We will touch on how Open Source platforms like Drupal and Mautic are well placed to deliver the digital experiences of the future, and jump in a time machine to get a glimpse of what lies ahead for a tighter integration between the two platforms.
