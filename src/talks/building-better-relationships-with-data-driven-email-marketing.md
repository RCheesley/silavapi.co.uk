abstract: >-
  As email marketers, getting our emails read by customers is our primary goal. While we have numerous technical ways to make sure we land in their inbox, whether they will actually read and engage with our content consistently over time comes down to how we have built and nurtured our relationship with them. How can we use data to understand our customers more deeply, personalise their experiences across multiple channels more effectively, and through this, build more meaningful relationships with them? Join this session to learn more! Note: 20 minute presentation.
